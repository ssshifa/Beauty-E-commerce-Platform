Homemade Hair Masks

  • Banana & Honey: Moisturizes dry hair.
  • Coconut Oil & Aloe Vera: Deep conditioning.
  • Egg & Yogurt: Adds protein and shine.
  • Avocado & Olive Oil: Repairs damage.
  • Fenugreek & Curd: Helps with dandruff.

DIY Face Masks

  • Turmeric + Yogurt + Honey: Brightening mask.
  • Oatmeal + Milk: Gentle exfoliation.
  • Multani Mitti + Rose Water: Oil control.
  • Cucumber + Aloe Vera: Soothing care.
  • Charcoal + Gelatin: Blackhead removal.

DIY Lip Care

  • Sugar + Honey: Natural lip scrub.
  • Beetroot Juice: Lip tint.
  • Ghee/Coconut Oil: Overnight softening.
  • Rose Petal Paste: Brightens lips.

Homemade Body Scrubs

  • Coffee + Coconut Oil: Smoothens skin.
  • Sugar + Olive Oil: Removes dead skin.
  • Rice Flour + Yogurt: Brightening scrub.

Extra DIY Beauty Remedies

  • Green Tea Ice Cubes: Calms puffiness.
  • Potato Juice: Reduces dark spots.
  • Lemon + Honey: Natural tan remover.
  • Apple Cider Vinegar: Skin pH toner (diluted).
  • Aloe Vera Gel: For acne, sunburn, dryness.
